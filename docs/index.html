Form — Training & Nutrition

An offline-first training and nutrition tracker for Android. Plan routines, train with guided rest timers, log meals and macros, and watch your progress grow — all your data stays on your device.

Everything a gym bag app should be

One app for the whole cycle: plan, train, eat, review.

🏋️

Guided workouts

Scheduled routines, a focused active-workout screen, automatic rest timers between sets, supersets and secondary exercises.

📚

1,324-exercise library

Every exercise with an animated demo, category, target muscle and equipment — plus full support for your own custom exercises.

🥗

Nutrition & macros

Meal logging with a built-in food database, calorie and macro tracking per day, and water intake.

📈

Progress that shows

Training history, personal records, charts and body metrics — see months of work at a glance.

🔒

Your data, your device

Everything lives in a plain-text vault (.md) in a folder you pick on your own storage. Export, import, read it with any editor. No servers, ever.
