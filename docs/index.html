Features

One app for the whole cycle.

Plan the week, train with a timer that has your back, log what you ate, and watch months of work add up.

Guided workouts

Scheduled routines and supersets, a fullscreen rest timer you tap to skip or log the next set, and values that carry to the sets after. A live notification keeps the timer on your lock screen.

1,324-exercise library

Every exercise with its target muscle, equipment, steps and a muscle map. Add your own exercises and tag them however you like.

Nutrition & macros

A meal library, calories and macros against targets made for you, water, and skipped meals — hold a meal to skip it.

Progress that reads at a glance

Week, month and year charts, sets per body part on a muscle map, and personal records — tap any day or month to drill in.

AI, with your own key

Training reviews, routines from a sentence, and meal estimates from a description or a photo. OpenRouter, OpenAI, Gemini and more — requests go straight from your phone to your provider.

Made for Android

Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile, app shortcuts, workout reminders, Health Connect, and light, dark or wallpaper colours.

Your data, in plain text

Routines, sets, meals and settings live in five Markdown files in a folder you choose. Sync them with Syncthing or Drive, open them in Obsidian, share a routine as text — no account, no server.

Ready when you are.

Free and open source. Download the APK and point Form at a folder.
